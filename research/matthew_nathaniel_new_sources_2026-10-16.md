# 2026-10-16 — Matthew, Nathaniel, early Maine and island-life discoveries

This research follows the merged PR #22 and contributes **new public historical profiles, seven dated story/timeline entries, two visitor stories, and a complete source-attributed 1865 family group**, not a claim that Matthew's parentage or the Scotland origin has been solved.

## 1. New public profiles: Nathaniel's family

- `nathaniel_beverage_1804_rockland` — Eaton, *History of Thomaston, Rockland and South Thomaston* II (1865), pp.146–147, describes a Rockland carpenter born around 1804, from Fox Islands, married to Sarah Alexander and a nephew of Capt. Matthew. No birth certificate, father's name or probate examined. The online compiled genealogy supplies **1803-10-20** and **1886-09-18** with parents John White Beverage and Rachel Lucy Crosby; these are **NOT added to the actual parent field**.
- `sarah_alexander_beverage_1808` — wife of the 1804 Nathaniel per Eaton. Eaton calls her a *niece* of Capt. Matthew. No parents are specified; compiled birth/death dates omitted.
- `henry_t_beverage_1841_rockland` — Eaton dates his birth **29 Nov 1841**, naming Nathaniel and Sarah as his parents. An independently dated 1898 Maine marriage index is referenced in the FamilySearch profile for his later wife Ella Allen, and her 1900 US census household reference identifies him. **Marriage-index and census page images have not been directly reviewed**.
- `ella_allen_beverage_1854` — later wife of Henry T.; the FamilySearch profile gives their marriage in Rockland on **17 Dec 1898** and birth/death years 1854–1919. These date years are provisional pending direct records. Do not infer she mothered any of Henry's siblings.

These profiles form a **standalone sourced household**, rather than a spurious link into the main direct lineage. The tree already has **Nathaniel Winslow Beverage (1776–1864), a different older person**. Eaton's 1804 Nathaniel must never be silently merged into him.

### Generational conflict

A compiled genealogical page at https://genealogyofnewengland.com/f_86c9.htm reports the 1803 Nathaniel as son of John White Beverage (son of Capt. Thomas) and Rachel Crosby. The 1865 Eaton account instead calls Nathaniel a *nephew* of Capt. Matthew. If Capt. Matthew were Thomas's brother, John White would be Matthew's nephew, making **Nathaniel Matthew's grandnephew**. Multiple explanations are possible (imprecise 19th-century kinship wording, a wrong proposed Matthew parent, or a misattributed Nathaniel) — **none is resolved**. Do not attach a father to Nathaniel or Matthew until an original record supports it.

## 2. Capt. Matthew's full published 1865 family, not yet a verified attachment

[Source, volume II pp.146–147](https://www.scribd.com/document/845728319/History-of-Thomaston-Rockland-and-South).

The author's precise place wording says Matthew was **born in Topsham or came from Topsham to the Fox Islands**; this is ambiguous. His two spouses are Abigail Dyer and Clarissa Cobb. Eaton describes **10 children (5 attributed to each wife)** and **11 named grandchildren of three sons**, while separately listing **three children of Nathaniel**. The full transcription-derived names and the source's birth/death qualifiers are preserved in `research/matthew_nathaniel_family_1865.json`. Visitors can browse them in the new Stories page. This is more accessible than an offsite PDF but avoids putting dozens of people whose identities have not been checked against original records into the main family tree.

The 1785 land petition's **minor Matthew** and **Capt. Matthew of Hope** are still **unproved as one individual**. No estate has been located or parentage established.

### Source and validation status

- Eaton's 1865 narrative is an **excellent near-contemporary named-family source**, not a nineteenth-century original vital/civil-register group. It is stronger than unsourced internet trees, but not independently conclusive.
- The surname-compilation page is **derivative**. Cross-reference does not by itself create two independent original witnesses.
- Ella Allen's FamilySearch [profile](https://ancestors.familysearch.org/en/GM6N-6CV) points toward indexed 1898 marriage and 1900 household record. **Original underlying record images still need inspection**.
- High-priority targets: Waldo County probate of Capt. Matthew, North Haven 1838 Nathaniel/Sarah marriage return, 1803 Nathaniel birth or John White's estate, 1898 marriage original for Henry/Ella, 1850–80 Rockland census.

## 3. A specific early Maine record: 1749 intention

[Brunswick Marriage Intentions, 1740–64](https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2008/06/brunswick-marriage-intentions-1740-1764.html), citing *Bangor Historical Magazine*, vol. 4 (1888), printed p.93, includes **James Beveridge and Jean White of Georgetown, 6 February 1749**.

The printed editor specifically says *intentions* precede actual local marriage returns from 1784; it does **not prove a wedding was solemnized on February 6, 1749** or uniquely identify the named James/Jean as the site's existing historical pair. The entry is now a dated timeline research event **without a `people_involved` link**. In particular, it should not be used automatically to alter the current family marriage dates or infer Matthew's baptism.

The previously cited Wheeler 1878 Topsham lot numbers for Samuel and James and possible Mercy marriage remain unproved as individual deed/marriage events.

## 4. Family lives and photographs: seven dated research events

- **1907:** [North Haven Historical Society, *A Free High School*](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/a-free-high-school.html) names Elsie and Jennie Beverage among the first five high-school graduates, at Pulpit Harbor Church. Names are unlinked to modern or similarly named profiles until identification.
- **1927-07-30:** [Courier Gazette original scanned newspaper](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/2a/Courier_Gazette_July_30_1927.pdf) announces an **August 24** auction of Sands H. Witherspoon's Pulpit Harbor homestead, with Frank Beverage as auctioneer. This item was already cited on Frank's research profile but not included in the event timeline; now the date/event is browsable. The newspaper is **not proof the sale actually occurred** or that Frank owned the property.
- **1937-10-15:** [Tonny Calderwood's diary, PDF p.29](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/1937.pdf#page=29) describes a golden wedding celebration for **Frank and May Beverage** with about 25 guests and reproduces the caption **from the back of a contemporary photograph**. The image itself has **not** been obtained or licensed; the connection to the site's Frank born 1861 requires proof of his marriage ~1887.
- **1967-03-11 and 1967-06-23:** [Tonny Calderwood's diary, PDF pp.22 and 54](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/1967.pdf) records a letter from *Sammy Beverage* requesting Calderwood family information and a later letter written by Tonny to *Samuel Beverage* with more details. It is **direct first-person evidence** of family-history exchange at that time. Whether the correspondent was the site's Samuel Hiram Beverage b.1917 is unverified; the event remains unlinked to a profile.
- **1975-11-13:** [North Haven Historical Society history](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/history.html), reprinting Lewis Haskell's 1994 recollection, attributes the recovery of original island deeds, road maps, first-bridge construction bills and images from the dump to Sam Beverage and neighbors; sixteen residents met November 13, 1975 to begin a society. The physical source materials' current box/call number are unverified. This clarifies a previous 1973 background event on Sam's profile without rewriting it.
- **1930s film:** [NHHS Videos](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/videos.html) hosts *Islands of Maine*, film shot by Harold Beverage of North Haven, Vinalhaven and Rockland circa 1930s, newly digitized. It cannot be assigned one exact recording date; it appears in an accessible story rather than a falsely precise timeline event.
