# Phase 12 — Mercy (Beveridge) Henry: grave evidence, reported marriage and an unplaced collateral branch

**Compiled 17 October 2026.** All source statements preserve the difference between a printed transcription of a gravestone and an original church marriage or ancestral probate record.

## Evidence retrieved

### 1. 1930 Maine Historical Society Topsham vital records, Volume II, under HENRY

Mary Pelham Hill (ed.), *Vital Records of Topsham, Maine to the Year 1892*, vol. II (1930), printed **p. 334** death list, reports the deceased as **Mercy [Beveridge]**, wife of James, died **26 October 1831**, aged **78 years 6 months**. The source marker **G.R.1** refers to a gravestone transcription; an additional **P.R.80** parenthetical alludes to Moses Woodman's *Early History of Topsham* among the listed private sources. The maiden name in brackets is an editorial/source-attributed expansion, not independently a marriage certificate.

- Digitized full-text edition: https://archive.org/stream/vitalrecordsofto00unse/vitalrecordsofto00unse_djvu.txt
- The printed cemetery-based age points approximately to **April 1753** as birth month, but the stone's exact physical wording and original birth record have **not** been inspected. Do not publish a precise day of birth.

### 2. Maine government First Parish Cemetery inventory

A statewide geographic cemetery inventory of **Topsham First Parish Cemetery** (Maine GeoLibrary PDF, Sagadahoc County, entries **139 / 139.1**) likewise lists **James Henry** died **2 Nov 1831** and his wife **Mercy (Beveridge) Henry** died **26 Oct 1831**, age **78 years six months**. Entry 139.1 supplies the maiden surname and spouse name.

- Direct governmental cemetery inventory: https://www.maine.gov/megis/pdfs/cemeteries/Sagadahoc/Sagadahoc%20County.pdf

**Critical source dependence:** This cemetery survey and the 1930 death list appear to draw on the **same underlying First Parish grave marker**. They are *not two independent death attestations*. The next step is to inspect the gravestone image, 1831 burial and probate documents and any other original record.

### 3. Wheeler 1878 — marriage to James Henry Jr. reported as probable

Wheeler & Wheeler, *History of Brunswick, Topsham and Harpswell* (1878), Henry family sketch, printed p. 837, names an earlier **James Henry ("the cooper")**, his son also **James Henry**, and suggests that the younger James **probably** married **Mercy Beveridge** on **15 February 1776**. That is a later family-history assertion, not the original solemnization record.

- Digitized family sketch: https://ldsgenealogy.com/ME/books/History-of-Brunswick-Topsham-and-Harpswell-Maine-including-the-ancient-territory-known-as-Pejepscot-part-79.htm

The grave-derived records lend support to Mercy as the wife of a James Henry at their deaths in 1831, but do **not** prove the exact 1776 wedding date or themselves distinguish every James Henry of that generation.

### 4. Local library narrative: proposed descendants (not yet independently established)

Topsham Public Library's First Parish Cemetery biographies name Mercy and younger James Henry and attribute to them a son **Capt. Robert Henry** (c.1779–1851). This is an interesting possible family branch, but the compiled narrative likely uses Wheeler, the cemetery inscriptions and other secondary source material. It is not an independently inspected birth or probate record proving the exact relationships.

- https://topshamlibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/12/1st-Parish-Church-Topsham-Cemetery-Life-Stories-Updated.pdf
- Topsham grave database likewise identifies Capt. Robert Henry and spouse Eunice, but these facts must not be conflated with **Robert Beveridge**, the 1776–1777 Topsham military officer (different name and apparent generation).

## Why this matters to our family tree

Mercy’s Beveridge maiden surname is attested by both the older Topsham history and a gravestone-derived record. She is a credible **unplaced historical Beveridge**, and her marriage may help unlock the Topsham kinship network. However, we have **no inspected primary document naming her parents**. She must NOT automatically be assigned as daughter of Samuel (1752), James (1758/1763), the Marblehead James (1724), or any Robert simply because the surname, dates and town overlap.

Her husband James Henry (reported death 1831) should not be confused with his own father **James Henry ("the cooper", death 1785)**. The public history card distinguishes them clearly.
