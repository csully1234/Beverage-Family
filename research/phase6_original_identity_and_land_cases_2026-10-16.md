## Main result: the two Frank Beverage identity trails

A previously cited **1954 North Haven inscription** and a **1939 Thomaston cemetery card** must not be automatically joined to a single person.

| Evidence | Date and access | What it directly says | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| North Haven autograph album | **19 July 1954**, [Heirlooms Reunited transcription and artifact](https://www.heirloomsreunited.com/2014/01/1880s1890s-autograph-album-of-north.html) | Signed Frank Beverage, aged 93, born **14 January 1861**, at North Haven | Site author's transcription of family artifact; original handwriting/signature should be checked |
| North Haven 43rd anniversary news | **18 October 1930** edition; [original Courier-Gazette PDF](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/pdf/270235292.pdf) | The NORTH HAVEN column reports that Mr. and Mrs. Frank Beverage celebrated their forty-third anniversary **Wednesday 15 October 1930** | Together with the independent 1937 fiftieth-anniversary diary, confirms how the couple publicly dated their anniversary; still not a marriage register and does not itself prove the full identity of the two spouses |
| Golden-wedding diary | **15 October 1937**, [Tonny Calderwood diary transcript, PDF p.29](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/1937.pdf#page=29) | Tonny attended his “Uncle Frank” and “Aunt May” **50th wedding anniversary**, about 25 present, and copied a caption naming Mr and Mrs Frank Beverage | Anniversary strongly suggests marriage about **15 October 1887**, but the marriage return/May's original surname and the couple's exact tree identity have not been recovered; photo *mentioned*, not obtained or licensed |
| Green Street household notice | **1 August 1911**, [original Courier-Gazette scan](https://fileserver-az.core.ac.uk/download/pdf/230340841.pdf) | “Mrs. Orra Roney and daughter Jeanette” visited “Mr. and Mrs. Frank Beverage, Green street.” | Town not established by quoted sentence; whether Orra is kin and whether this Frank corresponds to cemetery/another profile need household and vital records |
| Thomaston cemetery lot table | [Thomaston Village Cemetery plot-card transcription, section 8, lot 76](https://genealogytrails.com/maine/knox/cem_village_cards.html) | Frank Beverage is listed with the date **5 February 1939**; the same lot also names **Orra S. Roney**, along with other names | This is a *Date* field, NOT independently established death date or death certificate. No birth date or proof the 1911 Frank was this cemetery individual |

**Logical conclusion:** If the 1954 signature is dated correctly and the cemetery's 1939 date indeed indicates a burial/death, the two records cannot describe one person. The shared Orra Roney name is an appealing lead connecting 1911 social news to the Thomaston plot, **not a proved identity or relationship**. We have not added a 1939 death to the North Haven Frank b.1861 profile, or associated the burial/1911 Green Street events with that profile.

The **1930 newspaper and 1937 personal diary are independent dated accounts** of the anniversary, supporting **15 October 1887** as the couple's commemorated wedding date. This improves the earlier single-diary inference but **does not establish May's maiden name or the legal entry**. The 1930 article names the couple as Mr. and Mrs. Frank Beverage without spelling the wife\'s first name; that name comes from the 1937 diary.

The existing North Haven Frank profile already contains source-backed **1926 treasurer** and **1927 auctioneer** mentions. That information is retained; it does not prove the separate cemetery identity or 1937 wedding identification.

## Nathaniel of Rockland and Capt. Matthew of Hope

The previously used Scribd source is **replaced with a complete free public-domain digitization of the actual 1865 book**:

- [Cyrus Eaton, *History of Thomaston, Rockland, and South Thomaston*, vol. II, free Internet Archive reader](https://archive.org/details/historyofthomast02eato)
- [Direct complete 1865 PDF scan](https://archive.org/download/historyofthomast02eato/historyofthomast02eato.pdf)
- [Wikimedia Commons catalog and public-domain status](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:History_of_Thomaston,_Rockland,_and_South_Thomaston,_Maine,_from_their_first_exploration,_A._D._1605;_with_family_genealogies_(IA_historyofthomast02eato).pdf)

**Printed pages 146–147** name Matthew of Hope's spouses Abigail Dyer and Clarissa Cobb and ten reported children. The separately listed Nathaniel (b. around 1804), his wife Sarah Alexander and three children are described as nephew/niece of Matthew.

**Unresolved:** The original birth/baptism or estate identifying Nathaniel's father is absent. A derivative genealogy suggests Nathaniel was the son of John White Beverage and Rachel Crosby; if Matthew were Thomas's brother, this would imply *grandnephew* rather than *nephew*. Neither parentage nor the proposed Capt Matthew = 1785 minor identity is merged into the tree.

**Needed:** Nathaniel/Sarah 1838 marriage return (if extant); John White's estate/guardian entries; North Haven, Thomaston/Rockland original 1850–1880 federal census, and Matthew's Hope/Waldo probate around 1830–1845 (prior inquiry already sent, no duplicate requested here). Henry T. Beverage/Ella Allen 1898 original marriage return remains a distinct identity check.

## The 1888 Harrison dam — actual deed collection identified, deed not yet located

The [North Haven Historical Society Mill Stream exhibit](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/the_mill_stream.pdf) reports Harrison Beverage's **1888 lower-dam purchase**, but provides no original deed image in the examined material. This still does **not** establish that the purchaser is the already-profiled Harrison Beverage b.1825, nor an exact property area.

A genealogical catalog lists:
- **Knox County deed records, 1873–1917**;
- **Knox County deed grantor/grantee indexes, 1860–1920**;
- Additional original deed records 1791–1926.

[Record collection directory](https://www.ongenealogy.com/listings/knox-county-me-genealogy-records-online-at-familysearch/). The [Maine Registries of Deeds Association](https://www.maineregistryofdeeds.com/) hosts the official registry access portal. An additional [FamilySearch Maine wills and deeds collection](https://www.familysearch.org/en/search/collection/3158868) includes some original images, but a search result is **not** evidence of any particular Harrison transaction.

**Next:** look up Beverage and Beveridge in the Knox County grantor/grantee indexes for **1887–1889** and obtain the actual record book/page for the lower dam. Compare the deed boundary and names with the [original 1848 bridge company act](https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Laws/1848/1848_PS_c154.pdf), the [1883 Alvin Beach Bridge exhibit](https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/bridges.pdf#page=8) and the salvaged Pulpit Harbor map. **No ancestral parcel polygons, acreage, house addresses or false geocoordinates are added.**

## 1749 Brunswick James/Jean record: what remains unsettled

The [1888 transcription](https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2008/06/brunswick-marriage-intentions-1740-1764.html) records James Beveridge and Jean White, both of Georgetown, declaring an **intention** to marry on 6 February 1749. A [1895 transcription](https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2007/01/brunswick-marriage-intentions-1740-1769.html) reads James Beviridge and Jean Write, dated 6 February **1748–49**. These are **two transcriptions of one underlying register**, not independent evidence of a completed marriage. No dates, parentage, land title or relationships have been changed in base genealogy.

**Next:** obtain a scan of the original Brunswick marriage-intentions book, confirm Old Style date and name spellings, and seek a later solemnization or family estate record linking this couple to North Haven.
