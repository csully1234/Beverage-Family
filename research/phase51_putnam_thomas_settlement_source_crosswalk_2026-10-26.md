# Phase 51 — Thomas Beverage, Rufus Putnam's Fox Islands roster, and plan page 104

**Direct original:** owner's 8-page `4-29462-F012.pdf`, sha256 `a5baeec543e7c7f4a9e4cc0e32f3f2a6fb27257c0ea8606e10464c7ceeb8d404`, page 7. An exactly identical file was uploaded again as `4-29462-F012(1).pdf`; **this is not a second witness**.

## Primary-source reading and date interpretation

PDF page 7 has repeated handwriting column headings **Names** and **Time when Settled**. Under the Fox Island Division (labelled `Page 104` near the bottom of the left column, with continuation into middle and right columns) the middle names column includes:

| Transcribed original entry | Value | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thomas Beverage** | **15** | Duration of settlement reported in years under “Time when Settled”; it does **not** mean Thomas was fifteen years old |

The page is signed by Rufus Putnam **Boston, March 18, 1786**. The preceding six-page survey report contains a **Boston, December 16, 1785** signature. Since the underlying survey was carried out in 1785 and the supplementary report completed in March 1786, the safest date approximation for Thomas's stated settlement is **circa 1770–1771** (1785/1786 minus a rounded 15-year interval), rather than presenting 1771 as a precise arrival year. The report does not assign Thomas an individual lot or name his parents, spouse, siblings or birthplace.

**Scholarly reference:** David Flanagan and Barbara Beverage Reeves, “The Ebenezer Eames Family: Early Settlers of North Fox Island,” *The Maine Genealogist* 47(4) (Nov. 2025), printed p. 170 note 6 cites a Rufus Putnam report dated March 18, 1786, Box 001, Maine State Archives. The owner-supplied eight pages have that dated component but we **have not established the file's exact box/folio provenance or completeness** from an institutional archive confirmation.

## New cartographic cross-check: page 104

This manuscript does not simply say “Fox Island” without a map: it prints the heading **Fox Island Division — Page 104**. The Maine State Archives Fox Island plan metadata describes its map as `these islands delineated page 104`:

- Maine State Archives, *Fox Island Division of Islands, 1785*, Knox County Plan Book p. 7, digital item **302962**, a copy of Massachusetts Archives Maps and Plans #1343: https://digitalmaine.com/planbook_knox_county/8/
- 1839 *Land Agent's Report*, Schedule of Islands, printed page 63, again gives **page 104** for the **North Fox Island / South Fox Island** plan with grant date March 13, 1786 and John Calderwood et al.: https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Rpts/PubDocs/PubDocs1839v1/PD1839v1_00B.pdf

These independently described source records establish the **plan reference** and settlement geography. Page 104 is **not a lot number, property address, or proof of Thomas's specific allotment.**

## Complete page-seven working edition (not certified)

`research/phase51_putnam_page7_working_transcription.csv` contains **94 editorial rows across three manuscript columns**, including island headings, individuals, repeated possession claims and the final Putnam signature. **The number 94 is NOT a count of individual settlers**, and the CSV should not be converted into an automatically linked family tree or historical census.

Readings marked `low` are openly provisional; readings marked `medium` also require second examination. The original mixed **calendar years** (e.g. 1760/1763) with **durations** (e.g. 15/20 years) in its “Time when Settled” columns, and individual `do` marks repeat a preceding entry. Any public display must preserve that nuance.

Important visual landmarks near Thomas in the middle column:

- **Joseph Worcester — 20**
- **Thomas Beverage — 15**
- **Stephen Carver — 17**

Across the same page, two potentially family-looking but **unconfirmed** surnames appear with annotations of possession rights:

- John **B[e/r]verich** or similar [?] — 20
- William **Berich** or similar [?] — 13

These are **not verified Beverage/Beveridge occurrences**. A later, 1908 printed transcription of the 1790 census contains **John Babrick** and **William Barrick** as separate heads; the 1889 Vinalhaven history also prints William **Bassick** in the 1785 settlers list. These are useful spelling-comparison hypotheses, **not genealogy proof** or a basis to add new Beveridge people. Preserve the possibility that the handwritten names represent entirely different families.

## Three-era Thomas comparison without collapsing identities

| Record | Name as recorded | Evidence | Limitation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1785 petition, Massachusetts Archives, Series 228, ch. 97, original leaf 3 | Thomas **Beveridge** | Listed among original Fox Islands inhabitants | A name-list mention does not prove a signed attestation, lot ownership or parents |
| Putnam original March 18, 1786, uploaded p. 7 | Thomas **Beverage** | Reports 15 years' settlement in Fox Island division | Could be the petition signer; direct identity corroboration absent |
| 1790 federal first census (1908 published transcript, p. 32) | Thomas **Beverege** | Vinalhaven household with 2 free white males age 16+, 4 younger males and 5 free white females (11 persons) | Other household members unnamed; original manuscript uninspected and head's unique identity not proved |

1790 source: https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2006/10/1790-census-vinalhaven-hancock-county.html  
Original plan source: https://digitalmaine.com/planbook_knox_county/8/

The roster's wide overlap of surnames with the 1785 petition and 1790 Vinalhaven index (e.g. Waterman, Carver, Dyer, Cooper, White and Calderwood) strengthens the historical **community context**, not identity proof of particular kin. A direct deed, a guardian record, a grant allotment or corroborated original signature would be necessary to join people confidently.

## What the next archival step should be

1. Obtain the **original Fox Island Division plan numbered p. 104** and any *associated grantee/allotment list or proprietary division-book*, including image call numbers and individual lot references. The page 104 map is not itself an individual lot assignment.
2. Establish the **precise accession and extent of the eight-page Putnam report** and whether more Fox Islands claim lists follow the March 18 supplement.
3. Locate earliest Fox Islands **Thomas Beverage** property and tax records around **1770–1785**, including spelling variants; do not assume a 1786 grant proves ownership since 1770.
4. Locate the original **1790 Vinalhaven handwritten census** and identify the Thomas household's manuscript sheet and neighbor ordering. The 1908 printed table alone provides totals but not occupant identities.
5. Seek additional original records naming a relationship between Thomas, James and Matthew, without using the possible `minor / major [?]` note to assign an unproven birth year.

**Research standard:** No email sent; no presumed parent, child, date of birth, pre-Maine origin or exact lot included in canonical people records. The uploaded Putnam PDF remains a separate archival copy; only working transcriptions, citations and metadata appear in GitHub while its image-sharing provenance is unconfirmed.
