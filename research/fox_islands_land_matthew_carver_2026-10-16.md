# Fox Islands land, Matthew Beverage and Carver collateral-family research

**Research status:** 16 October 2026. All relationships below are distinguished between **documented text**, **later compiled genealogy**, and **unproven cross-source identifications**. Do not modify the original Boston Tea Party claim, family addresses, or existing relatives' records based on this research.

## 1. 1785–1786 land petition and original plans

The *Brief Historical Sketch of the Town of Vinalhaven* (1889), printed pages around 9–11, and Clifford N. Carver's *The Carver Family of New England* (1935), printed pp. 96–98 (attached 244-page scan PDF pp. 114, 117–118), reproduce the same 1785 petition and March 1786 Massachusetts resolve. They are **two later transcriptions of the same historic claim**, not two independent eyewitness accounts.

Three relevant names are in the enumerated inhabitants: **James Beveridge; Thomas Beveridge; Matthew Beveridge (a minor)**. George White and John Calderwood, **as attorneys for the inhabitants**, are the two names beneath the transcribed signature notice. Do not describe the three Beveridges as autograph signatories without consulting the original manuscript.

The resolve states that a 1785 survey found **16,527 acres across the Fox Islands group**, seventy-five pre-1784 settlers, a conditional grant to qualifying settlers and successors, and land reserved for a ministry and school. These are **total island / collective grant figures**, not Beverage family landholdings. A claimant's inclusion does not establish an individual deed, surveyed lot acreage, or exclusive ownership.

## 2. Matthew Beveridge: potentially missing branch; identity remains unproven

**A)** 1785 petition transcription: a **Matthew Beveridge**, described as a *minor*, is a named claimant. A later secondary tree assigns 1766 as a proposed birth year, which would fit minority in 1785, but this is **not a primary birth record**.

**B)** Cyrus Eaton, *History of Thomaston, Rockland, and South Thomaston* (1865), volume II, **printed pp. 146–147**, describes **Capt. Matthew Beverage of Hope**, associated with Topsham and Fox Islands, and records:

- First wife **Abigail Dyer of Fox Islands**; children **Harrison** (lost at sea), **Achsa** (married Charles Heald), **Louisa** (died young), **Mary Miles** (married Anselm Tucker), **James Madison** (born circa 1813, married Mary J. Taylor).
- Second wife **Clarissa Cobb**; children **Ezekiel G. D.** (married Martha Gilmore), **Sarah Abby** (died young), **Thomas Jefferson** (born circa 1820, married Deborah M. Taylor), **George W.** (born circa 1824, married Martha Frances Piper), **Martha W.** (married Charles L. Allen).
- Separately, a **Nathaniel Beverage, born 1804**, is described as *a nephew of Capt. Matthew*, with wife Sarah Alexander and children Henry T., Mercy and Frederic. **This does not disclose which sibling of Matthew was Nathaniel's parent.**
- Later genealogy suggests a **Matthew** was a child of **James Beveridge and Jean White** and a sibling of Capt. Thomas Beverage, but the connection among A/B/this genealogy is **not yet independently proved**.

**Important date conflict:** The current family archive records James Beveridge Jr. as dying on March 1, 1785. The copied March 1785 petition includes James Beveridge. This is an unresolved historical/date/person-identification issue, not grounds to change the website's death record. A submitted claim need not indicate the person personally signed on the final petition date.

**Research queue:** Obtain Capt. Matthew's Hope probate estate (circa 1837, date unverified); look for Topsham/Bath birth and baptism ~1766; original marriage to Abigail Dyer and later Clarissa Cobb; Hope/Rockland censuses and deeds; legal guardianship/land claim records of named minor; an authenticated probate link to James and Jean; separate parentage for nephew Nathaniel.

**Decision:** Show this as a **promising, documented candidate branch in research**, not as an automatically merged new set of tree relationships.

## 3. Carver family and land at Carver's Harbor

Clifford N. Carver (1935), printed p. 108 / PDF p. 130, states **Thaddeus Carver** purchased approximately **700 acres and a sawmill** at what became Carver's Harbor on South Fox Island/Vinalhaven for £260 from Francis Cogswell; describes a home still family-occupied in 1932. This is a published account requiring the original deed before reconstructing legal lot lines or acreage on a map. It is **Carver property, not Beverage property**.

The existing Beverage Family tree identifies **Olive Leadbetter Carver (1829–1909)** as Albert Glover Beverage's spouse and Israel Carver Jr.'s daughter. The 1935 book documents several Carver generations on Fox Islands, but no matching marriage/parentage was verified in the book for *that* Olive. Do not lengthen her ancestor chain by assuming a same-named Israel belongs to this genealogy.

**Research queue:** 19th-century Olive/Albert marriage record, Olive's birth record and father's probate, Israel Carver Sr./Jr. identification, old Cogswell→Thaddeus deed and any later Carver-to-Beverage land transfers.

## 4. Early map chronology appropriate for visitor atlas

Original image files in the user's `All_NH_Maps.zip` were cross-matched to the [Holmgren historic map catalogue](https://holmgren.org/NorthHavenHistoricalMaps/). The atlas now makes the following **ten** images readable by pan/zoom, while keeping the existing modern-place map:

- 1776 Des Barres naval chart: Pulpit Cove and Kent Cove settlement context; not person-specific.
- 1859 Chace North Haven/Vinalhaven: named homes including Beverage labels; not title boundaries.
- 1859 North Haven village inset: Henry Beverage / Josiah Beverage 2nd directory.
- 1859 Carver's Harbor inset: Vinalhaven business and settlement context.
- 1867 Dorr North Haven: 1:10,000 detailed structures, walls and fields.
- 1868 Fox Islands Thoroughfare survey: later edition with navigational updates to 1886.
- 1873–74 northern offshore islands: potential offshore reference geography.
- 1890 Stuart atlas: transport roads including period after 1883 bridge.
- 1901 USGS revision, printed 1904: pre-summer-estate transition view.
- 1941 USGS survey, printed 1944: comparison later century.

**Do not georeference historic house-name labels to a specific 2026 private home/address from visual guesswork.** Especially avoid presenting walls and modern addresses as authenticated Beveridge deed lines.
