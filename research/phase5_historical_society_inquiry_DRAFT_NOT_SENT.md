**Subject:** Research inquiry — 1883 Alvin Beverage bridge contract, 1937 Frank and May photograph, and recovered Pulpit Harbor records

Hello North Haven Historical Society Archives,

I've been researching the Beverage/Beveridge families of North Haven and building a family-history website connecting original historical records, photographs and maps to individual family profiles.

Your online exhibits have been especially helpful. I'm hoping to identify and, where possible, obtain copies of a few underlying items, with proper credit and permission if anything is eventually reproduced on our family site.

1. Your nine-page *Pulpit Harbor Bridges* PDF (https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/bridges.pdf) reproduces an 1883 town contract involving **Alvin Beverage** near PDF page 8 and later original payment records near page 9. Do you have collection numbers, a transcription, or clearer images of the **1883 contract** and related town bills? I'm especially interested in the original wording and dates, the identification of Alvin, and any accompanying maps or land descriptions.
2. The *Mill Stream* exhibit (https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/the_mill_stream.pdf) refers to **Harrison Beverage's 1888 purchase of the lower dam**, the mills operated by **Thomas and James Beverage**, and the early map/sketch recovered by Sam Beverage. Do you know the **original 1888 deed book/page** or where the cited old survey/sketch is held?
3. The account of the Society's 1975 founding mentions a salvaged **Pulpit Harbor road map, bills for the first bridge and old schoolhouse-lot deeds** (https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/history.html). I'd appreciate collection/box references and whether any of these documents have been scanned.
4. The **October 15, 1937 entry** in Tonny Calderwood's diary mentions **Frank and May Beverage's 50th wedding anniversary** and records a caption from a photograph of them (https://www.northhavenmainehistoricalsociety.org/uploads/5/2/8/7/5287313/1937.pdf#page=29). Does that original photograph survive in the Calderwood papers, and is there any identification of May's maiden name or the couple's actual wedding date? I'm not asking to reproduce the photograph without the owners' permission.

If any of these records can be accessed or scanned, I'd be grateful for guidance on how to obtain them and the proper citation and publication credit. Please let me know any copying fees or reproduction restrictions **before** any paid work is undertaken.

I'd be happy to share the family-history website and specific references if useful. Thank you for preserving these remarkable North Haven records.

Best regards,

Cade Sullivan
