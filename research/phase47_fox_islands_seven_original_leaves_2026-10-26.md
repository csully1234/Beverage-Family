# Phase 47 — the original 1785 Fox Islands petition and 1786 resolution

**Investigation date:** October 26, 2026.  
**Scope:** Seven original Massachusetts legislative leaves supplied to the project owner as TIFF files: four petition leaves and three resolution leaves, plus seven companion phone photographs. The original scans were directly viewed in this investigation. **The TIFF/JPEG files are not yet committed to the website**, and this text is not a substitute for those images.

## Why this changes our evidence base

The website's Phase 30 source-access register, prepared October 19, described the Massachusetts legislative originals as not yet obtained. That state is now obsolete. The supplied originals, labelled `SC1 228 Passed Resolves 1785 ch97`, establish an actual *contemporary manuscript* source rather than reliance solely on the 1889 printed town history.

### Exact original name locations

| Manuscript location | Reading | Limits |
|---|---|---|
| Petition, leaf 3, left column | James Beveridge | A name on a community petition list, not yet proof of signed original attestation or a specific freehold |
| Petition, leaf 3, right column | Thomas Beveridge | A name on the same list, not proved identical to a separately documented town clerk |
| Petition, leaf 3, right column near bottom | Matthew Beveridge, with `a minor` annotation | A designated minor; parentage, age, legal capacity, and later identity unknown |

**Critical distinction:** These three names appear in the manuscript's list of inhabitants; the individual list entries must not be described as independently authenticated handwritten signatures by each person. The petition identifies George White and John Calderwood Jr. as attorneys for the inhabitants. The minor designation and placement do **not** establish that James or Thomas was Matthew's father or guardian.

### Facts established by inspecting the 1786 resolution

- Senate document dated **March 11, 1786**, with House approval marked **March 13, 1786**. The phrase `Resolves 1785` in the archival file series refers to the legislative session, not a claim that both documents were signed in 1785.
- The survey as recited in the resolution covered **16,527 acres** of the Fox Islands division in Penobscot Bay.
- The text recites **72 settlers before January 1, 1784**. This is **not** the same tally as later histories' 75 inhabitants/petitioners; the categories differ. No individual Beveridge qualifying settlement date is established here.
- A **conditional** grant was made to John Calderwood and other qualifying settlers, their heirs and assigns, with **200 acres for the ministry** and **200 acres for a grammar school**.
- Purchasers of an original settler's improvements could inherit the corresponding rights under specified circumstances. Listing someone's name does not establish they retained a particular parcel or paid all conditions.
- Monetary passages and all docket markings have not yet been independently verified word-for-word; they are not silently reconstructed in the site manifest.

### Complete seven-leaf review queue

1. **Petition 1:** Early settlement assertions, forced wartime absence, property and livelihood losses.
2. **Petition 2:** Reoccupation, public worship and schooling aspirations, property request, attorneys, first inhabitants.
3. **Petition 3:** Two-column list with James, Thomas and minor Matthew Beveridge; June 1, 1785 endorsements.
4. **Petition 4:** Committee report and exterior docket not yet fully deciphered.
5. **Resolve 1:** Survey acreage, settlement cutoff, grants, public-land reservations and financial conditions.
6. **Resolve 2:** Purchasers' improvements rights; House and governor's approvals.

Selected transcription fragments are saved in `data/phase47_fox_islands_original_manuscripts.json`; this edition deliberately flags **all seven leaves as NOT fully transcribed or peer-checked**.

## Three source families, not three votes

The actual 1785–1786 manuscript is the primary source for name occurrences. The 1889 printed *Brief Historical Sketch of the Town of Vinalhaven* reproduces the same petition. Later derivative reprints are aids to transcription, **not independent records proving kinship**. Separately, the 1785 survey copy in the Maine State Archives establishes geography and its own date and provenance, but cannot identify Beveridge parcels by itself.

The November 2025 *Maine Genealogist* research by David Flanagan and Barbara Beverage Reeves cites original settler residence declarations in the March 18, 1786 report (Maine State Archives, Box 001). It reports individual arrival histories for Eames settlers; **this investigation has not accessed an individual Beveridge declaration**.

## Highest-payoff original records, ranked

1. **March 18, 1786 Putnam report, Box 001, Maine State Archives:** inspect *every* Fox Islands settler entry under Beveridge/Beverage variants, including any age, settlement-year declaration, acreage, original lot number and spelling.
2. **North Fox Island allotment/deed chain, 1786–1820:** use Lincoln County and successor jurisdiction deed indexes. Pair grantee, grantor, neighbors and survey description. The two institutional survey copies are not proofs of independent family settlement.
3. **Contemporary minor/guardian records:** check whether Matthew was represented through a legal guardian, and compare inheritance or appointment proceedings without imposing a modern minimum-age assumption.
4. **Vinalhaven plantation clerk minutes 1785–1789:** directly compare Thomas's manuscript signature to the petition's *list hand*. Distinguish clerk records from individual petition signatures.
5. **Later Hope Matthew documentation:** seek a deed or family/guardian record that explicitly bridges geographic or familial identity to the 1837 probate decedent.
