# Phase 52 — Revolutionary-era group petition and Nathaniel Beverage's 1818 pension certification

**Research date:** 2026-10-26. **Status:** Source-led historical notes; not proof of an individual Revolutionary War service record for Thomas Beverage.

## A. Fox Islands settlers' 1785 petition: collective wartime claim

**Primary source:** Massachusetts Archives, SC1 / Series 228, Passed Resolves, February 1785 session, chapter 97, petition leaves 1 and 3, as preserved in the website's seven-leaf facsimile series.

- Leaf 1 recounts settler flight after British troops took Bagaduce and says inhabitants who relocated under Commonwealth protection "did serve during the Expeditions to Penobscott bearing Arms against the enemy". The passage also alleges that families lost property and some inhabitants were forced to work on British fortifications.
- Leaf 3's two-column inhabitant list includes **Thomas Beveridge** (right column), **James Beveridge** (left column), and **Matthew Beveridge** (right column). Their entries appear in the *same petition*, but not directly under individual declarations of service.
- **Inference boundary:** The service allegation is collective. The list includes a widow and people marked minors, and it must not be treated as a nominal roster of soldiers. Thomas's inclusion **does not prove** he bore arms, was at Bagaduce, served on a particular vessel, or participated in the 1779 American expedition.
- The handwriting's personal names are list entries, **not individually authenticated signatures**. The identity of the listed Thomas with Capt. Thomas Beverage of later records is plausible but requires independent corroboration.

## B. 4 May 1818: Nathaniel Beverage certifies a Revolutionary War pension claimant

**Primary source:** Original 1818 declaration of **Theophilus Hopkins** of Vinalhaven, NARA copy supplied via FamilySearch, film/image group **116138577**, image **17 of 62**. [Open exact image](https://www.familysearch.org/ark:/61903/3:1:3QHN-T7M5-9911-8?view=fullText). The uploaded exhibit is titled `Transcript (4).pdf`.

- The upper portion records Hopkins's first-person claim of service during the Revolutionary War and his poverty; details of regiment and enlistment need cautious manuscript checking because the automated transcription is visibly unreliable.
- The lower certification is headed **"Vinalhaven May 4th 1818"** and is by "Selectmen and Overseers of the Poor". It identifies **Theophilus Hopkins** as commonly reported to have been a private in the late Revolutionary War and in need of relief.
- Two name-signatures are visibly present: **Nathaniel Beverage** and **Zebulon Standly** (the second surname is also printed as *Stanly* in the 1889 roster).
- This is a **direct contemporary name occurrence** and civic-office/documentary-attestation record for a Nathaniel Beverage. It is **not evidence that the signer, his proposed father Thomas, or another Beverage was a Revolutionary veteran**.

**Independent cross-check:** The 1889 Vinalhaven roster of officers, printed pp. 35–37, lists for **1818**: clerk George Dyer; treasurer Nathaniel Beverage; selectmen **Ebenezer Calderwood, Nathaniel Beverage, Zebulon Stanly**. The close match of the two signers and the year corroborates their official capacity. [Transcribed officer roster](https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2007/01/vinalhaven-town-officers-1790-1889.html). The roster is a later compilation, while the 1818 certification is contemporary.

**Likely identity:** Nathaniel Winslow Beverage (1776–1864), listed as a son of Capt. Thomas Beverage in the American Ancestors Boston Tea Party biography, is a plausible match to the Vinalhaven officeholder. However, this particular document gives no middle name, age, parent or spouse and does **not alone establish that identification**. Do not add a new canonical genealogy link without independent bridging evidence.

**Independent archive trail:** Maine State Archives' Digital Maine collection lists *Revolutionary War Pension application — Hopkins, Theophilus (Vinalhaven)*, identifier **29479**, described as a Hancock County Supreme Judicial Court application, broad creation date **1819–1820**: https://digitalmaine.com/revolutionary_war_hancock_county/81/ . This is a **related source target**, not proved to be the same page or part of the same file until contents are directly compared. The differing catalog date must not silently be equated to the 1818 certificate.

## Priority research conclusions

1. **New evidentiary advance:** the 1785 petition gives a contemporary *community-wide* armed-service claim in a document explicitly naming Thomas Beveridge. This is stronger evidence of the community context than the 1889 reprint, **not individual proof**.
2. **New genealogical source:** a 4 May 1818 certification, with an apparently contemporaneous Nathaniel Beverage signature, corroborated by the printed 1818 town-officer roster.
3. **Outstanding:** Find individual 1779 Penobscot Expedition muster/pay or privateer records; obtain the March 1865 Albert Beverage letter in full; verify the identification of the 1818 signer; examine all pages of the 1819–1820 Hopkins application for collateral evidence.
4. **Website integrity:** Keep any Thomas combat/privateering claims explicitly *unverified*. Do not claim that Nathaniel's pension certificate grants Nathaniel or Thomas military service status. Do not merge the two Hopkins source records until originals compared.
