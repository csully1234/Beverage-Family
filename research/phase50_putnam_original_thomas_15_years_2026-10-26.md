**Examined:** October 26, 2026

## The breakthrough

**PDF page 7 contains a contemporary original-manuscript settler observation:**

| Detail | Directly read |
| --- | --- |
| Manuscript section | A supplementary list of the **Fox Island division of islands**, within an overall roll of Deer Island, Isle of Holt, and Fox Island divisions |
| Printed image/PDF page | **7 of 8** |
| Source heading | *Names* / *Time when Settled* |
| Name | **Thomas Beverage** (as written; not silently replaced with 'Beveridge') |
| Entry | **15** |
| Unit | **years**, established by adjacent explicitly written "years" entries and the column meaning |
| Report signing | **Boston, March 18, 1786 — Rufus Putnam** (at the foot of p. 7) |
| Calendar interpretation | **approximately 1771** (1786 minus the reported 15-year duration), **not an exact year, day, or birth** |
| Genealogical identity | Named original-era historical settler; **not automatically merged with Capt. Thomas Beverage or the 1785 petition Thomas** |

This observation is a significantly stronger record of a particular Thomas's *self- or surveyor-reported occupation duration* than prior 1889 retrospective printing. It is NOT proof that he first arrived exactly in 1771, nor that he was the same Thomas later documented in the first census.

### The supplied manuscript's exact title and structure

The manuscript opens with a long title substantially reading:

> Description of the Quality of the Land Surveyed for the Commonwealth of Massachusetts in the County of Lincoln 1785, by Rufus Putnam, Jonathan Stone, Samuel Titcomb, and John Mathews, with the Names of Inhabitants found on them, and time of settlement.

This title is a **selected visual reading**, not yet an independently checked letter-by-letter diplomatic transcription.

- **PDF pp. 1–3:** Descriptions of land quality and townships, plus some residents' original settlement years.
- **PDF p. 4:** Heading *A List of Inhabitants found on the Islands or having improvements on them*, naming other eastern Penobscot-area islands.
- **PDF p. 5:** *List of Inhabitants on Deer Island*, with two tables of individual settlement dates.
- **PDF p. 6:** Continued geographic survey, signed *Boston December 16th 1785* by Rufus Putnam. It says the **Deer Island, Isle of Holt, and Fox Island divisions** were not yet completed and a list of settlers would be added when ready.
- **PDF p. 7:** The supplementary list of remaining islands, including the **Fox Island Division page 104** reference; the table continues across columns, with Thomas Beverage and the number 15. A footer reads *Boston March 18th 1786*, signed Rufus Putnam.
- **PDF p. 8:** Back-of-leaf annotations and numerical workings, not fully transcribed.

The March 18, 1786 date **matches** the report referenced in *The Maine Genealogist* (47 no. 4, Nov. 2025), which cites an original report of Rufus Putnam in Maine State Archives paper Box 001. **Important archival qualification:** The PDF does not itself authenticate its present physical shelf mark, and the supplied eight pages may be an extract from a larger report. We should verify with the archive before describing this exact file as the complete contents of Box 001.

## Comparing the growing historical chain

| Date | Record | What it establishes | What it does not |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1785 | Massachusetts original settlers' petition, SC1 / Series 228, ch. 97 | Thomas **Beveridge** named among the Fox Islands inhabitants | Parentage, precise lot, personal signature authentication |
| Mar. 18, 1786 | Original Putnam settlers' supplementary list, supplied PDF p. 7 | Thomas **Beverage**, reported **15 years settled** | Exact arrival date, family relationship, identity with a specific Thomas profile |
| 1790 | Printed later transcription of original Vinalhaven census | A **Thomas Beverege** household, 2 males 16+, 4 under 16, 5 females (11 total) | Names or relationships of the other 10 people; proven identity with 1786 Thomas |
| Dec. 20, 1819 | Benjamin Beverage contemporary island letter, Maine Memory Network no. 20801 | His historical recollection of local island settlement and economy | Parents or land parcel of 1786 Thomas |

**Do not collapse these four differently spelled, differently dated names into a single provable person just because the island is the same.** They are a strong research chain deserving comparison and original-deed investigation, not yet a genealogy-proof chain.

## New avenues made possible

1. **Early 1770s land/title investigations:** Now search roughly 1768–1775 for evidence involving Thomas Beverage, not only 1785–1786 and later postwar grants. Search land improvements, island rates, survey notes and neighboring allotments in Lincoln County and Massachusetts land-office holdings.
2. **Isle of Holt / Fox Island division geography:** The new original page references other island divisions and numbered survey pages (including Fox Island division, page 104); identify exactly which sheet contains Thomas's claimed improvements before mapping anything to North Haven today.
3. **Identifying the Thomas of the 1785 petition:** Compare the 1785 name, 1786 “15 years” observation, 1786 Thomas sworn statement in the Paul Reed/Duglass litigation, 1790 household, and 1785/1789 plantation clerk appearances. Strong circumstantial grouping is not direct identity proof.
4. **Name reading audit:** A nearby p. 7 name that might appear to resemble `Beverich` is *not safely identified as a Beveridge/Beverage member*. Get a second original reading and independently located surname evidence before entering it as a family member.
5. **Exact archive metadata and image rights:** Confirm the holding institution, Box 001 accession, missing pages, and permission to publish derivatives of this newly uploaded report. No public page image has been added by this Phase 50 commit because the PDF's archive-copy provenance and image-use permission have not been established.
