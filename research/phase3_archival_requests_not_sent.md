# Early Beveridge genealogy — targeted archival requests

**Prepared 16 October 2026. Drafts only; NOTHING HAS BEEN SENT.** These are specific, narrow records requests. Seek permission or fees before commissioning scans. Do not send any outgoing mail until explicitly approved.

## 1. Maine State Archives — original Putnam settlers' declarations (highest priority)

**Contact:** maine.archives@maine.gov
Official access: https://www.maine.gov/sos/archives/services-and-records-management/about/maine-state-archives-hours-and-contact-information

**Subject:** Fox Islands settlers' declarations — Rufus Putnam report, 18 March 1786 (Box 001)

Hello Archives Services,

I am researching the first Beverage/Beveridge families who settled the Fox Islands (now North Haven and Vinalhaven). A citation in David Flanagan and Barbara Beverage Reeves, “The Ebenezer Eames Family: Early Settlers of North Fox Island,” *The Maine Genealogist* 47, no. 4 (November 2025), p. 170, note 6, identifies an original paper record:

“General Putnam’s Report and Description in the County of Lincoln with an Estimate of Lands Surveyed There in 1785—Townships Between Schoodic and Cobscook Rivers and Between Cobscook and Passamaquoddy Bays,” dated March 18, 1786, **Box 001 (paper)**, Maine State Archives, Augusta.

The article reports that Fox Islands settlers declared how many years previously they had settled. Would you be able to confirm that this item contains the Fox Islands settlers' statements, and advise whether a digital scan, photograph, or transcription of the relevant pages can be obtained? I am especially interested in James, Thomas, and Matthew Beveridge (including any age/minor notation), their neighbors, and any accompanying lot/acreage description. If that record has related field notebooks, indexes, or later grant/allotment returns, their call numbers would also be very helpful.

I have the digitized 1785 Fox Island Division map from Knox County Plan Book p. 7 (https://digitalmaine.com/planbook_knox_county/8/), but understand the requested 1786 report may be a different record.

Please let me know about reproduction options and any fees. Thank you for your assistance.

## 2. Waldo County Probate Registry — Capt. Matthew Beverage (circa 1830s)

**Contact:** registerofprobate@waldocountyme.gov
Official: https://www.waldocountyme.gov/m/directory/department?did=11
**County filter:** Hope was in Waldo from 1827 to 1860. Do not use Knox's *1861–1915* collection as a substitute.

**Subject:** Historical Waldo probate index search — Matthew Beverage/Beveridge, Hope, ca. 1830–1845

Hello Register of Probate,

I am researching an early Knox/Waldo-region resident, Capt. Matthew Beverage (also spelled Beveridge), described as a resident of Hope, Maine, in Cyrus Eaton’s *History of Thomaston, Rockland, and South Thomaston*, volume II (1865), printed page 146. A later compiled genealogy suggests a death around 1837, which I have not verified.

As Hope was part of Waldo County during that period, could you please advise whether the historical probate index for approximately 1830–1845 contains an estate, will, administration, guardianship, or inheritance proceeding for Matthew Beverage/Beveridge (including variant spellings)? If so, I would appreciate the book/page or file number, reproduction options and any fees. Related family names in the published account include wives Abigail Dyer and Clarissa Cobb, and children Harrison, Achsa, Louisa, Mary Miles, James Madison, Ezekiel, Sarah Abby, Thomas Jefferson, George W., and Martha W.

If the older files are in a different repository, I'd be grateful for a referral.

Thank you.

## 3. Massachusetts Archives — June 1785 Fox Islands petition and allotments

**Contact:** archives@sec.state.ma.us
Official: https://www.sec.state.ma.us/divisions/archives/research/research-services.htm

**Subject:** 1785 Fox Islands land-title petition and 1786 grant / original claimant schedules

Hello Reference Staff,

I am looking for original documents associated with the Fox Islands (North Haven and Vinalhaven) settlers' petition presented around 1 June 1785 and the Massachusetts General Court's conditional grant of 13 March 1786 (Rufus Putnam survey). The petition is reproduced in the 1889 *Brief Historical Sketch of the Town of Vinalhaven* and names James Beveridge, Thomas Beveridge, and Matthew Beveridge (a minor), along with other settlers. It appoints George White and John Calderwood as attorneys for the inhabitants.

Would you be able to identify the manuscript petition, legislative file, and any Eastern Lands Commission records, grants, individual lot/allotment schedules or deeds containing the names and lands held by those settlers? I'm particularly interested in an original document date, any reference number, and scan or visit options. I have consulted your Eastern Lands finding aid and the Maine State Archives' digitized 1785 island-group plan, but have not found named individual parcels.

Please advise how best to order scans, if available, and any fees. Thank you.
