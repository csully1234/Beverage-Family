# Collateral family expansion — 2026-08-31

This pass expands sideways from already-established Beverage ancestors rather than accepting surname matches.

## Admission rule

A new profile is included only when its connection to an existing anchor is supported by a published genealogy close to the family period, a contemporary obituary, a municipal vital-statistics report, or a combination of those sources. Cemetery indexes corroborate dates; they are not used alone to invent relationships.

## Privacy boundary

Living spouses, children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren named in modern obituaries were not converted into new profiles. The source trail may mention that living descendants exist, but no new private contact or location data is recorded.
