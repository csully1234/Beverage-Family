# Collateral family expansion — 2026-08-31

This pass expands sideways from already-established Beverage ancestors rather than accepting surname matches.

## Admission rule

A new profile is included only when its connection to an existing anchor is supported by a published genealogy close to the family period, a contemporary obituary, a municipal vital-statistics report, or a combination of those sources. Cemetery indexes corroborate dates; they are not used alone to invent relationships.

## Privacy boundary

Living spouses, children, grandchildren and great-grandchildren named in modern obituaries were not converted into new profiles. The source trail may mention that living descendants exist, but no new private contact or location data is recorded.

## Leads intentionally held back

Several Beverage/Beveridge names occur in Fuller Cemetery and online compiled trees but were not added because this pass did not establish a reliable parent-child or spouse bridge to an existing profile. The large James Beverage (born about 1805) / Waterman collateral line is also held back until James's exact connection to the established Capt. Thomas Beverage tree is proven from a record rather than inferred from surname and location.


## Verified phase-two expansion

This pass adds another source-first layer after the initial 22 collateral profiles. It resolves Sarah Beveridge (1727) from Marblehead vital records, repairs obsolete Thomas/Sarah/Abigail child IDs instead of creating duplicates, upgrades the Samuel Carver Beverage–Ruth Leadbetter twelve-child generation to exact published dates, and extends documented deceased collateral lines through Fremont/Alida Thurston, Harold/Petronėlė Jurgeliūtė, Elroy Victor Beverage Sr., and Helen Beveridge Barclay.

Modern obituaries were used to prove historical/deceased relationship chains, but living children, grandchildren, current addresses, phone numbers, and other modern personal details were deliberately not turned into profiles.

- **Benjamin Kent / Sarah Beverage parentage conflict:** chronology strongly suggests an older Sarah rather than the repository’s younger Sarah profile; this remains a documented conflict pending an original vital/probate/deed record.
