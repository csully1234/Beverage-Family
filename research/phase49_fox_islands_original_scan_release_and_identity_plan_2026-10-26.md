# Phase 49 — Publish the actual 1785–1786 Fox Islands manuscripts and chart the next proof chain

Research and source-publication date: **2026-10-26**.

## The visitor experience materially changes

The Fox Islands original-document exhibit now includes **seven optimized original manuscript page images**, derived from the seven project-supplied high-resolution TIFFs. The source file names are retained in \`data/phase47_fox_islands_original_manuscripts.json\`. This is a significant improvement over the October 26 research-only Phase 47/48 release: visitors can study the handwriting *in context*, select any page, jump between neighboring pages, download a single page, or export the complete indexed seven-page JPEG set.

Eleven image files are now part of the repository, at \`static/approved_media/fox_islands_1785/\`: seven full leaves plus four detail excerpts (James, Thomas, Matthew, and Matthew's disputed age note). **These are photo-derived manuscript images, not generated illustrations or facsimiles reconstructed by AI.**

Each page has an original-TIFF SHA-256 recorded in the data manifest. The original TIFFs (27–30 MB each) are deliberately **not** committed to GitHub, to preserve repository and deployment efficiency. Keep the separate complete research ZIP for full-fidelity archival access. The optimized website images are ~2400 pixels high; they are useful for reading, but not a substitute for full original resolution when paleographic detail matters. Reproduction rights/credit language should be confirmed with Massachusetts Archives; archival attribution is already visible on the page.

Historical manuscript source: **Massachusetts Archives, SC1 / Series 228, Passed Resolves, Resolves 1785, February Session, Chapter 97**. Four petition leaves plus three resolve leaves; legislative action reached its House approval in **March 1786**.

## The original names do not establish kinship

| Source leaf | Name | Direct observation | What is NOT established |
|---|---|---|---|
| Petition 3, left | James Beveridge | Named in two-column list | Parentage, age, legal land title, identity with a canonical James |
| Petition 3, right | Thomas Beveridge | Named in same list | Whether identical with Capt. Thomas, Thomas of 1790, or Thomas from 1786 testimony |
| Petition 3, right | Matthew Beveridge | Named; underlined "a minor" plus separate possible "major" lower note | Legal age, parents, exact relationship, identity with Matthew of Hope who died 1837 |

**Caution:** Names in a collective petition **are not authenticated independent signatures** of the individuals. The age note beside Matthew is genuinely ambiguous and remains open to professional paleographic review.

The March 1786 handwritten resolve refers to **72** settlers before January 1, 1784 in a surveyed land division of **16,527 acres**; a *later published transcription of the same clause* prints **75**. This is a conflict between editions, not a merely different population definition. Both readings are displayed. The cause is unresolved.

## Where this intersects with already-indexed sources

The site's **Phase 45 early island census evidence** already contains the 1790 printed Vinalhaven entry for **Thomas Beverege**, with 2 males >=16, 4 boys under 16 and 5 females (11 people). This discovery **predates** Phase 49 and is now cross-linked to the original 1785 image viewer. No person besides the household head is named by that row. The second older male could be a relative, hired worker, or other resident; **do not identify him with James or Matthew from the petition without documents**.

1790 printed federal source, Vinalhaven town, printed p. 32: https://archives.mainegenealogy.net/2006/10/1790-census-vinalhaven-hancock-county.html

The site's Phase 39–45 research also already identifies a **28 June 1837 Thomas Beverage to James Beverage and others deed**, Hancock County Deeds p. 387, FamilySearch DGS 8296915, image 207/601 (via the Wilford Woodruff Papers' specific archival citation). This could be especially important for continuity across generations, but the documented identity and death-chronology conflicts remain unresolved; a reference to a deed cannot prove the grantor's age, whether living, or the grantees' kinship without original examination.

Wilford Woodruff Papers (biographical source and primary-image footnote): https://wilfordwoodruffpapers.org/subjects/josiah-beverage

## Exact outstanding document targets — prioritized

### 1. March 18, 1786 Rufus Putnam report (the breakthrough target)

Maine State Archives; paper **Box 001**. The precise archival description is supplied in **David Flanagan and Barbara Beverage Reeves**, "The Ebenezer Eames Family: Early Settlers of North Fox Island, Maine," *The Maine Genealogist* 47(4) (November 2025): 170–182, p. 170 footnote 6:

> General Putnam's Report and Description in the County of Lincoln with an Estimate of Lands Surveyed There in 1785 ... 1786-03-18.

The article reports declarations of years since settlement for **Mark and Justus Eames** and other island settlers, demonstrating that this record type preserves relevant information. It does **not** give a verified individual arrival declaration for any Beveridge in the accessible passage. Request the original complete Fox Islands claimants' list and context folios, **all spellings**, settlement dates, lot names/numbers, neighboring claimants, and any minors/guardianships.

Publisher-hosted legal excerpt/source: https://maineroots.org/images/Nov25TMG2.pdf (printed p. 170, PDF page 28, zero-index 27).

The **Maine State Archives launched its new Open Access digital repository October 1, 2026**, https://digitalarchives.maine.gov/. It may improve discovery of land and manuscript originals. **No matching scan of Putnam Box 001 has yet been confirmed.** Official announcement: https://www.maine.gov/sos/news/maine-state-archives-offers-expanded-digital-access-open-access .

Current Maine State Archives email from official contact page: **maine.archives@maine.gov**. Neither an archival email nor any paid scan request has been sent.

### 2. 1800 Vinalhaven 'from whence emigrated' manuscript entry

Read the original special information preserved in the 1800 Hancock census and compare Walter Goodwin Davis, "Part of Hancock County, Maine, in 1800," *NEHGR* **105 (1951):204–213, 276–291**. The unusual recorded places of origin may reveal Thomas or other Beverage households' pre-island location. The cited article says its printed summary condensed some original fields, so read original names, ages and full entries; do not infer that Beverage was from Marshfield based on Eames neighbors.

Cross-reference: https://wiki.whitneygen.org/wrg/index.php?title=Archive%3ANEHGR%2C_Volume_105 ; 2025 article cited above.

### 3. Chain of Fox Island grants and conveyances

- 1785 plan: Maine State Archives, Knox County Plan Book p. 7, digital identifier **302962**: https://digitalmaine.com/planbook_knox_county/8/
- 1839 State public-land schedule lists **North Fox Island, 5,281¼ acres**, purchaser/grant to **John Calderwood et als**, dated March 13, 1786; this is for the overall island group and **not a Beveridge parcel**.
- Seek initial claimant allotment books, Lincoln County deed indexes and post-county-change record chains. Preserve **neighbors, lot bounds, parcel bearings and grantees** separately and do not draw a guessed lot on a modern GIS map.
- 1914 Maine Forest Commission report reproduces the text of the March 1786 grant and references later resolves **February 19, 1787** and **June 17, 1791**. These may alter title treatment; check the original resolves rather than treating the late reprint as contemporaneous.

1839 official source: https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Rpts/PubDocs/PubDocs1839v1/PD1839v1_00B.pdf .
1914 official printed retrospective reference: https://lldc.mainelegislature.org/Open/Rpts/PubDocs/PubDocs1914Bv2/PD1914Bv2_12.pdf .

### 4. Matthew's family-identity bridge

Request professional reading of both age notes on original petition leaf 3; check 1785–1790 minor or guardianship references and deed covenants. The 1837 Hope probate establishes a widow and named children but, without a link to the Fox Islands record, does not join the two Matthews.

### 5. 1790–1837 documentary succession

- Verify 1790 Thomas *Beverege* original census categories against the 1908 government printing and identify his nearby listed household heads cautiously. Being consecutive census entries does **not** prove contiguous land.
- Verify 1774 Trinity Church Thomas–Mercy marriage against the original Boston register (FamilySearch DGS 8130800, image 459/798).
- Read 1837 Thomas-to-James deed image DGS 8296915, frame 207/601 and examine legal capacity, parcel and witnesses; do not infer which Thomas was grantor before addressing chronological conflicts.
- Read 1820 Vinalhaven M33 roll 34, sheets 657–664, for named heads John, Nathaniel, Thomas and Benjamin; the indexed list is not itself an original handwritten observation.

## Status policy and release notes

- This phase publishes *evidence*, not new pedigree relationships. No parents, siblings, ages, deeds or migration towns are inferred.
- Claims based on the 1889 transcription have been clearly distinguished from the newly hosted contemporary original.
- The 2025 journal article stays at its publisher, not rehosted.
- The 1790/1837 sources were **already found by earlier project phases** and are presented here as cross-referenced targets rather than false new discoveries.
- No original full-page TIFFs were deleted or replaced. No private credentials, customer data, passwords or archived PDFs were changed. No archival emails were sent.
- Public release requires tests on GitHub and confirmation that the manual Render deployment is live.
